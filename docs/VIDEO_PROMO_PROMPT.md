
# OrarioDoc AI - Video Promo Prompt Pack
**Obiettivo:** Creare un video trailer di 60 secondi per il lancio dell'app.

Questo documento contiene i prompt da utilizzare con strumenti di **AI Video Generation** (OpenAI Sora, Runway Gen-3, Luma Dream Machine, Kling) per produrre le clip necessarie al montaggio.

---

## 🎨 Stile Visivo Generale (Style Reference)
> **Prompt di Stile da aggiungere a ogni scena:**
> "Cinematic 4k footage, highly detailed, warm lighting, shallow depth of field. Modern UI interface overlays in Material Design 3 style (purple and teal neon accents) floating in the air. Professional commercial look, shot on ARRI Alexa."

## 🎬 Sceneggiatura e Prompt per Scena

### Scena 1: Il Caos (0:00 - 0:10)
*Contesto:* Un insegnante sommerso dalla burocrazia cartacea.
*Voiceover:* "La didattica non dovrebbe essere solo carta e scadenze."

**Prompt Video AI:**
> "Close-up shot of a tired teacher's desk overflowing with disorganized stacks of paper grades, registers, and sticky notes. Dusty light streaming through a window. The camera slowly pans over the chaotic mess. High resolution, realistic texture of paper."

### Scena 2: La Soluzione - Big Bang (0:10 - 0:15)
*Contesto:* L'insegnante prende il telefono, apre l'app.
*Voiceover:* "Scopri OrarioDoc AI. Il tuo nuovo copilota."

**Prompt Video AI:**
> "A modern smartphone screen held by a hand. On the screen, a sleek purple app icon pulsates. Suddenly, the screen erupts in a digital particle effect (Big Bang animation), transforming into a clean, organized dashboard with 'OrarioDoc AI' logo. Sci-fi UI transition, glowing interface."

### Scena 3: Assistente Vocale Live (0:15 - 0:25)
*Contesto:* L'insegnante cammina in classe e parla al telefono.
*Voiceover:* "Parla con i tuoi dati. Voti, note, lezioni: basta la tua voce."

**Prompt Video AI:**
> "Medium shot of a smiling teacher walking in a modern blurred classroom background, holding a phone. A holographic waveform overlay appears near the phone, pulsing in sync with speech. The UI on the phone shows 'Listening...' and then instantly fills a digital grade book. Futuristic HUD effect."

### Scena 4: Progettazione Intelligente (0:25 - 0:35)
*Contesto:* Focus sulla Timeline Gantt e l'analisi dei documenti.
*Voiceover:* "Dalla burocrazia alla strategia. L'AI costruisce la tua programmazione."

**Prompt Video AI:**
> "Macro shot of a tablet screen displaying a beautiful, colorful interactive Gantt chart timeline. Digital blocks rearrange themselves intelligently. A sidebar shows an AI scanning a PDF document icon, extracting glowing lines of text and converting them into lesson plans. M3 Expressive design style."

### Scena 5: Il Futuro in Classe (0:35 - 0:50)
*Contesto:* Analisi dell'apprendimento con grafici.
*Voiceover:* "Monitora i progressi, non solo i voti. Inclusività e analisi reale."

**Prompt Video AI:**
> "Over-the-shoulder shot looking at a laptop screen showing sophisticated analytics charts (radar charts and trend lines) about student performance. The charts animate and grow. The lighting is soft and professional. The interface is clean, using pastel purple and teal tones."

### Scena 6: Outro (0:50 - 1:00)
*Contesto:* Logo finale e Call to Action.
*Voiceover:* "OrarioDoc AI. Insegna meglio, vivi meglio."

**Prompt Video AI:**
> "Clean studio background. The text 'OrarioDoc AI' forms from light particles in the center. Below it, the text 'Scarica Ora' appears. The background is a smooth gradient of deep violet and soft pink. High-end motion graphics."
